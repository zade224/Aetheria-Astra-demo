Ayan — extracted animation frame pack

Folders contain individual 192x192 RGBA PNG frames extracted from the Ayan sprite artwork.
Animations: idle, walk, run, jump, attack_1, attack_2, attack_3, skill_gale_piercer,
ultimate_tempest_awakening, damage_hurt, die.

IMPORTANT: These are automatically cropped and background-segmented frames from generated artwork.
They are a usable prototype asset pack, but edge cleanup and frame alignment may still be needed
before polished production use. Animation timing and game integration are not included.
